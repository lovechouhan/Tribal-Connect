About TribalConnect

India's premier tribal e-marketplace — connecting indigenous artisans with conscious buyers to preserve ancient crafts, empower communities, and celebrate heritage.

✦ Our Mission

Empowering Tribal India, One Craft at a Time

TribalConnect was born from a deep belief — that every tribal artisan deserves a fair marketplace. We bridge the gap between indigenous craftsmen in remote tribal regions and buyers across the globe who value authenticity.

Every product on our platform carries a story of culture, tradition, and community. When you buy from TribalConnect, you're not just purchasing a product — you're sustaining a livelihood.

Our Core Values

🤝

Fair Trade

Artisans earn directly with no middlemen. 100% of the listed price goes to the creator.

🌿

Sustainability

All products use natural, forest-sourced materials. Eco-friendly by tradition.

🏛️

Heritage Preservation

Every sale keeps a centuries-old craft tradition alive for future generations.

💡

Digital Empowerment

Bringing tribal artisans online — simple tools, multilingual support, direct payments.

🇮🇳

Government Supported Initiative

TribalConnect is aligned with India's Van Dhan Vikas Kendra (VDVK) scheme under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and TRIFED. We support tribal SHGs, artisan cooperatives, and GI-tagged products from across India.
